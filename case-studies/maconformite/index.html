MaConformite.ma — Building a workspace for LBC-FT compliance

MaConformite.ma helps Moroccan firms organize anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing (LBC-FT) work, from finding a company to reviewing its dossier.

Full-stack developer at Inforisk · Figma, Angular, and Spring Boot

My role and the team

I worked with the project team and collaborated with UX experts on parts of MaConformite.ma. My role as a full-stack developer spanned Figma, the Angular interface, and the Spring Boot services behind it.

The screens below trace how company lookup, client onboarding, and ongoing review fit together in the workspace.

The problem

A compliance team needs to find the right company, collect the required information, and understand what to do next. The workspace pairs a shared overview with dedicated screens for each step.

Start from the dashboard

The dashboard combines company search with a snapshot of the existing portfolio. A reviewer can look up a new company or scan the risk and alert panels before returning to a dossier.

01 Find the company

The search supports company name and registry identifiers such as ICE and RC. Results show the company’s location and registry details, giving the reviewer a way to confirm the match before opening a dossier.

02 Review the KYC questionnaire

The reviewer can complete the Know Your Customer (KYC) questionnaire in the workspace or send it to the client. The two options make it clear who supplies the information and where that work starts.

See what the client completed

Once the questionnaire comes back, each section shows its completion state. The reviewer can see which information is available and continue to the supporting documents from the same screen.

03 Review the dossier

After the review, the dossier shows the decision alongside the KYC status, latest screening, and next review date. Bringing those details together gives the team a clear point of reference when they return to the relationship.

What I’d Measure Next

In task-based testing, I’d track how long it takes to find the correct company, whether reviewers can complete a dossier, and where they need to backtrack or ask for help.

What I Learned

Working across design and development made it easier to spot where the interface depended on backend rules. A status only helps when it also tells the reviewer what happened and what they can do next.
