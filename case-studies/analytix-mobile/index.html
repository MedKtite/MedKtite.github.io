Analytix Mobile

Company intelligence and risk monitoring on Android and iOS

Analytix is Inforisk’s mobile app for checking Moroccan companies. It brings company records, risk indicators, compliance checks, and portfolio alerts into a mobile workflow.

Mobile developer at Inforisk · Figma, Flutter, Dart, Spring Boot

01 Find a company

Start with a company name, then open the matching profile. The company record gives users a place to confirm the legal identity and registration details before they interpret its financial or risk data.

02 Read the risk signals

The company menu gives users access to reports and risk views from the profile. The default risk screen pairs a score with a plain-language interpretation and a chart that shows how the score changed over time.

03 Monitor changes

The shareholders view brings ownership information and access to compliance screening together. The alerts feed helps users spot new events affecting companies they follow.

My role

At Inforisk, I took the mobile app from early Figma prototypes to production. I built the Flutter interface, connected it to Spring Boot REST APIs, and implemented push notifications for portfolio updates.

The mobile work included managing search, results, company detail, and alerts across the shared Android and iOS codebase. I also handled configuration, versioning, and release submissions for both stores.

From build to release

I shipped the app for Android and iOS. The app brings company lookup, risk review, and ongoing monitoring into one mobile experience.

What I’d measure next

I’d test how quickly a user can find the right company, whether they can explain what the risk view shows, and how often an alert leads them back to the relevant record.
